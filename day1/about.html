About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas, reminders, and tasks before they disappear. It helps you keep your notes organized so you can quickly find and review them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the New note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your saved notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • Keep your notes organized in one place.
  • Simple and easy-to-use note management.
  • Works directly in your web browser.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save the current note
